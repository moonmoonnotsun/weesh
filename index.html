The problem

Wishes die in a notes app.

Most people can name five things they have meant to do for years. Try climbing. Learn to dance. See Japan. The next step is usually already for sale nearby: a class, a ticket, a trip. They miss it because they are not searching the week it's bookable. Search answers when you ask. A notes app keeps the sentence. And the wish quietly expires.

The product

From a sentence to a Saturday.

01

Say it in your own words.

“Try padel”, “I want to climb”, “Find great ramen”. Weesh saves the sentence exactly as written. It asks one question only when the plan would be wrong without it, like whether you're new to climbing.

  • Your own words
  • One question, max
  • No chat to scroll
02

It looks for one real step.

Not ten options. One place that fits the wish, picked from a checked catalog of real venues. Each wish shows an honest status: Searching or Step ready.

  • One place per wish
  • Only real places
  • Never a filler
03

Friday, 17:00. Your week arrives.

Three steps, shown like stories. What's ready for your wishes this weekend: the place, how far it is, the hours, and where the info came from.

  • 3 steps a week
  • Distance & hours
  • Source on every card
04

Go, or pass.

Get directions and go. Or say “too far”, “not this”, “later”, and the wish goes back to looking. Done wishes become your month in review, the thing people share.

  • Directions in one tap
  • “Not this” is fine
  • Month recap

Product principles

Built on six promises.

No feed.

There is nothing to scroll. The list is the product. With no wishes, Weesh has nothing to show you.

1

One step, not ten options.

Choosing is the hard part. One place per wish. Pass on it, and the wish looks again.

Still looking

“Still looking” is an answer.

If nothing real fits yet, the wish waits. No stand-in suggestion just to look busy.

✈︎

Japan waits for a real flight.

Big wishes don't get swapped for “visit a museum this weekend”. They wait for a step that actually moves them.

It can't make places up.

The model can only return an id from a checked catalog. Every card shows where its info came from and when.

The list stays on the phone.

No account needed. Wishes and the visit log live on the device. Weesh never books or pays on your behalf.
