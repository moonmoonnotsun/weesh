05 / Why this is not a tarpit

“Things to do nearby” is a guess.

Timeleft matched people on shared dreams. It made no money. The founder said dreams stay dreams. One concrete outing format later: ~€18M ARR at $19.99 / month.

  • Your wish, not inferred taste. Discovery has to guess. We start from the sentence.
  • One step, not the perfect place. A place, a price, a link. Or it waits.
  • The day it is real. Not a weekly dump of options. The wish stays open until the step exists.
