Problem

People save experiences. They rarely follow through.

  1. DiscoverMaps, notes, social apps, friends.
  2. SaveThe idea is kept. The plan is not.
  3. ForgetNo time, no next step. The wish goes quiet.

Sheeran & Webb, 2016: intentions fail without a concrete plan. Arival & Phocuswright, 2026: people who book an experience ahead usually already knew what they wanted.

Insight

The missing layer is between intention and action.

  1. IntentionSomething they already want to do
  2. WeeshA real opportunity, at a moment they can act
  3. ActionThey go

Product

One saved wish becomes one plan.

  1. 01 Say it In their own words. Weesh keeps the wish.
  2. 02 Weesh finds one real place Only places it already knows. If nothing fits yet, it keeps searching.
  3. 03 They go A place, a time, and the venue’s own link.

Why now

A vague wish can finally be tied to a real place.

  1. AI reads intention. “Yoga, beginner, near me” becomes a local plan, and only matches places Weesh already knows.
  2. Live search is too costly for a free app. Grounding with Google Search costs $14 per 1,000 queries. A shared city catalog stays cheap.

Landscape

Others help people discover. Follow-through is still open.

Ask Maps, ChatGPTAnswer when you ask. They do not keep a wish from January.
Pinterest, Instagram, TikTokKeep the post. The plan is still yours to build.
GetYourGuide, ViatorSell ticketed tours. Strong at checkout.
Fever, EventbriteShow what is on sale now.
TimeleftOne format: dinner with strangers. ~150k monthly users, €18M ARR on their site.
WeeshKeeps the wish until one real place is ready.

Why not Google? Google answers the question you type today. Weesh remembers the wish you wrote in January and comes back when there is a plan.

Business model

Paid when people go. Then paid to keep going.

First

5-10%

Commission on the step

When the plan is bookable, like a class, a ticket, or an experience, Weesh earns a referral. Public programs in this category commonly pay 5-10%.

Next

$7-10/mo

Subscription

For people who come back. Timeleft already sells one planned outing a week at $19.99.
